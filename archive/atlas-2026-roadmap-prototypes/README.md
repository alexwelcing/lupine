# Superseded 2026 roadmap prototypes; not current plans

These files are early design and roadmap prototypes for the viewer and an
in-house simulation engine (`ATLAS-architecture.jsx`, `ATLAS-presentation.jsx`,
`manifesto/`, and the `atlas-view-*.jsx` mock-ups). They were moved here from
`atlas/` on 2026-11-11. Nothing builds or serves them; their targets and
performance figures are not Lupine results. The maintained LUPI viewer lives in
the standalone `alexwelcing/Lupi` repository.
