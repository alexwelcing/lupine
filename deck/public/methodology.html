Methodology

Name the error. Close the loop.

Atomistic simulations are only as useful as our account of where they fail. Lupine's methodology treats prediction error as a geometric object — measurable, tiered, and reusable as a training signal.

The working paper

The Causal Geometry of Prediction Errors in Interatomic Potentials

Our working paper introduces a unifying language for MLIP error: a causal/structural decomposition that maps prediction failures back to the physical configurations that caused them.

The paper is currently in preparation. We do not describe it as published, submitted, reviewed, or accepted. It is being developed in the open alongside the atlas-distill audit engine and the Lean formalization of its core accuracy commitment.

The companion manuscript extends the same framework to interatomic-potential benchmarks, showing how error-geometry constraints propagate from density-functional training data through to molecular-dynamics trajectories.

The audit loop

Four steps, each testable.

The methodology is not a black-box score. It is a loop that produces evidence at every stage.

01 — Predict

Run the simulation

Execute the MLIP or DFT workflow on a well-defined benchmark geometry and record the full prediction vector.

Verified
02 — Compare

Compute the error field

Measure force, stress, energy, and relaxation residuals against a trusted reference. Error is treated as a field over configuration space.

Verified
03 — Decompose

Name the cause

Cluster failures by local structure, composition, and trajectory history. The geometry of error becomes a diagnostic, not just a number.

Triangulated
04 — Correct

Retrain or restrict

Route the failing configurations back into training data, guardrails, or runtime fallbacks. The loop closes.

Claim tiers

Every public number carries a label.

We do not let a single impressive metric stand in for rigor. Each claim on this site is tagged by how it was produced.

Tier What it means Example
Verified Reproduced by an independent run or external benchmark on the same data. CHGNet-vs-DFT force RMSE on a named test split.
Disclosed Our own measurement, with method and assumptions listed explicitly. Atlas-distill runtime on a local GPU lane.
Triangulated Cross-checked against two or more distinct sources or models. Agreement across CHGNet, MACE, and ORB on a failure mode.
Projection Model-based extrapolation from current evidence, with assumptions stated. 5-year DCF or scenario-weighted valuation.
Directional A qualitative thesis or conjecture, not yet quantified. "Scarcity is an engineering problem."
