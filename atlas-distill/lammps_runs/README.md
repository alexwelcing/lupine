# Third-party interatomic potentials and LAMMPS runs

The potential files in these directories (`*.eam.fs`, `*.eam.alloy`, `*.meam`
and similar) were published by other authors and are included only so the
LAMMPS runs here can be reproduced. Each file's header credits its authors;
most were obtained through the NIST Interatomic Potentials Repository. They
keep their original authors' terms and are not covered by this repository's
MIT or CC BY 4.0 licences. See `../../NOTICE.md`.

The `log.lammps.*` files are outputs of our runs with those potentials.
