## Concrete primary-government archive

[NIMS Creep Data Sheet service](https://cds.nims.go.jp/) identifies systematic standards-based creep/rupture measurements for heat-resistant steels and Fe-, Ni- and Co-base superalloys, and lists material categories and related creep-strain/metallographic resources. It cites [Sawada et al., Catalog of NIMS creep data sheets, DOI 10.1080/14686996.2019.1697616](https://doi.org/10.1080/14686996.2019.1697616). The old [service address](https://smds.nims.go.jp/creep/) directs readers to this current endpoint. This is a primary national-institute measurement program, not a composition/property scrape from miscellaneous MPEA papers.

A potential task is recorded rupture-life optimization under fixed stress/temperature with a jointly recorded ductility or rupture-elongation constraint. The project's earlier operating-condition source audit documents heat/product/processing and rupture-property concepts in the catalog. This reconnaissance confirms the current service and measurement categories, not actual paired row availability. Exact usable counts, specimen-level pairing, temperatures/stresses shared by candidate heats, uncertainty, strain/elongation semantics, source/heat grouping and overlap with model development are unverified. Different tests at different stresses/temperatures cannot be treated as a common candidate panel by merely calling them creep records. Archived new-to-project data are not new experiments or automatically independent of prior corpora.

## Access and licensing: present blocker, not a ready open-data run

The current landing page explicitly requires DICE account registration, email-domain registration and service application. It forbids bulk data acquisition by manual or mechanical methods and prohibits web scraping under the MatNavi terms. No login, application, contact, data-sheet request or bulk collection was attempted. The page also prohibits unauthorized copying/distribution of the database system; a redistributable observation-data license has not been established.

Crossref metadata for the catalog paper reports CC BY-NC 4.0 for the version of record. That paper license does not grant unrestricted bulk access or redistribution of the separate NIMS database. Do not apply a government/publication label as an open-data license. This lead is therefore not executable under current metadata-only work. A future authorized access route or explicitly licensed archival subset would be required before custody acquisition; no request or paid experiment is authorized by these notes.
