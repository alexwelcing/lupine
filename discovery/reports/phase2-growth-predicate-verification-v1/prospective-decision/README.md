## Minimal projection after parsing succeeds

Freeze a complete documentary mapping followed by one complete non-outcome
frame/provenance roster, with coordinates bound before selected text disclosure.
These are two dependent phases of the same readiness decision, not per-cell releases.
The mapping needs endpoint definitions/units (including uniform versus total elongation),
reference-key semantics, row/test/aggregate definition, condition inheritance and
missing/censored/not-applicable codes. Mixed documentary/measurement/example/cache
cells block the affected disclosure; digit stripping cannot establish a documentary role.
Any necessary bounded shared-string decoding must be explicitly scoped and logged.
“No numerical-performance extraction” does not mean the machine never sees target-bearing XML.

The roster must retain **all original framed rows**, stable private row-to-test identity,
reference joins and supported specimen/heat/batch/campaign/repeat links, condition-scope
metadata and unresolved records. Structural presence is separate from numerical validity;
nonempty is not a usable pair. Distinguish headers/blanks/notes/summaries and reconcile
version/field/row meanings behind the retained 1,038/1,050/1,070 claims rather than trimming
to a count. Preserve article-local citation IDs: the known CR49/CR50 DOI collision forbids
DOI-only union. Compare lineage to authenticated target-redacted exposed-source manifests,
keeping composition reuse, measurement/campaign reuse and computational provenance distinct.
No endpoints, target hashes, numerical ranges, ranker scores, thresholds, allocations or
model/split choices belong in this projection.

End with a named decision: metadata supports a specified finite recorded-label frame,
or pairing/frame/meaning/use terms remain unknown or refuted and that claim stops.
Numerical completeness still needs a separately frozen later rule before an audit;
this projection cannot certify it. A census of fixed archived labels does not require
physical independence. Fresh-holdout, panel, IID, unique-evidence-cost and plant-validity
claims need additional premises and remain blocked when unsupported. Parsing success
alone closes none of those scientific gates.
