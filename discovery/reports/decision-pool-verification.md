## Implemented behavior

`decision_pool.py` binds a declared protocol, complete candidate universe,
supplied ranking permutation, feature identity, calibration panel identities
and recorded outcomes. The score is the maximum normalized rank of **all**
true feasible tied minimizers, with zero for empty or infeasible panels.
Calibration uses exact rational ceiling and order-statistic arithmetic.
Selection receives no future truth. The prefix includes its boundary.

Missing labels preserve the original panel count and force whole-universe
fallback without a calibrated threshold. Unsupported sampling also abstains.
An unavailable rank uses the score's mathematical upper bound one, retaining
every candidate. These outcomes remain distinct. A finite zero prefix means
an empty statistical pool, never certified infeasibility. No feasibility,
incumbent, regret, or hard physical interval certificate is produced.

## Limits and preserved negative controls

The retention guarantee is marginal over calibration and one future panel.
A constructed exchangeable collection has marginal retention `19/20` while
retention conditional on a feasible panel is zero. Another has marginal
failure `1/2` but conditional failure one for a realized calibration score.
Both are exercised by independent runtime tests. Wrong best-optimum scoring
and strict boundary exclusion are also detected.

None of this establishes a useful smaller pool, a feasible recommendation,
physical reliability, source independence or new scientific superiority.
The frozen JARVIS screening/utility failure, failed ridge alternatives, and
original three empirical-interval failures remain unchanged. New scientific
use needs a frozen panel protocol and unused evaluation outcomes.
