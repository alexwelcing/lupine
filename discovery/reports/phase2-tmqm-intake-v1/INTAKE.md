## Required bindings before audit outcomes

- **Source and rights:** exact release/files and byte custody; primary unit/key
  definitions; application of the repository MIT declaration to the data and
  upstream CSD-derived material. Article terms cannot resolve upstream rights.
- **Task and estimand:** objective/feasibility functions and units, directions,
  ties and missing-value policy; candidate universe and decision-panel meaning;
  recommendation loss and intended claim. Thresholds need an independent
  decision rationale or separately frozen development rule, never favorable
  audit outcomes.
- **Complete frame:** stable record/panel IDs, duplicate and structure handling,
  method-specific eligibility and explicit exclusions. Preserve every record's
  disposition; freeze the residual audit population and size. Do not drop
  unfavorable or missing cases after evaluation.
- **Information and cost:** allowed features and their acquisition/derivation
  costs. Orbital energies and gap are directly related: an unseen-gap prediction
  must not quietly receive target-revealing HOMO/LUMO quantities. Audit related
  thermodynamic identities and optimized-geometry dependence. xTB and DFT
  information are distinct resources.
- **Separation and overlap:** fixed development/calibration/audit roles, lineage,
  policy and preprocessing before audit labels; grouping/overlap treatment for
  CSD ancestry, structures, ligands, metal families and derived tmQMg/tmQMg-L.
  A new filename does not reset exposure or prove training exclusion.
- **Equal-budget baselines:** prespecified nominal ranking, random ordering and
  a justified scoped baseline, with identical candidate universe, available
  information and per-panel budget. Define whether one query returns a property
  or a joint vector; account for shared calculations, features, fitting and
  tuning. No hidden extra labels or free expensive features.
- **Evaluation rule:** fixed count/allocation/seeds/stopping, primary/secondary
  metrics and multiplicity; all-tied-optimum retention, feasible recovery, pool
  size, loss/regret and costs as appropriate. Freeze success, negative,
  insufficient-resolution and abstention states. Preserve failures; no
  retrospective threshold, budget or metric selection.

## Inference and next action

A **fixed-archive audit** concerns recorded calculated labels in a complete
frozen finite population. Uniform sampling without replacement does not require
physically independent molecules, but does require fixed labels/policy, a valid
frame and the stated sampling mechanism. A planning bound is not observed
coverage or power. A molecule row is not automatically a decision panel.

**Prospective generalization** to new chemistry, calculations, experiments or
power-plant conditions requires separate evidence and a justified sampling or
shift model. Group labels alone establish neither independence nor
exchangeability. Archived DFT/xTB labels are not experimental truth; no new DFT
is required or claimed for the archive audit.

Next, only bounded, separately authorized documentary/header readiness work
should resolve the missing bindings. The prepared header-only operation has
**no reviewed result in this intake**. If a public result arrives, retain its
exposure/limits without promoting labels into semantics or admission. Resolve
units, keys and rights from primary definitions. If mandatory premises remain
unavailable, retain NOT_REGISTERED/NOT_ADMITTED. Actual allocation follows a
complete reviewed freeze; this card does not initiate it or retry the denied
