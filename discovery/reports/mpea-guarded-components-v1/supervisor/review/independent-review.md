The independent logs contain no sensitive sentinel.

The leader-peak test deliberately replaces the RSS sampler with zero observations while the real synthetic child allocates and releases 96 MiB; wait4 still detects a peak above its 64 MiB synthetic cap. A separate unmodified-sampler case detects resident memory above the same cap. This distinguishes the mechanisms rather than claiming every transient aggregate peak is detected.

Limits remain material: aggregate RSS is sampled on a nominal 10 ms loop, can miss inter-sample peaks, can double-count shared pages and is subject to scheduling delays. wait4 supplies the leader's kernel peak, not the simultaneous aggregate descendant peak. This is not an instantaneous 2 GiB process-tree ceiling, address-space limit or cgroup measurement. Cleanup covers observed process groups within the worker session; deliberately detached new sessions and supervisor SIGKILL are outside its cooperative guarantee. Child filesystem reads are not syscall-isolated. ACK ordering constrains the cooperative worker protocol; it cannot prove a hostile worker has not decoded data early.

The supervisor accepts closed stage markers but does not verify the underlying scientific artifacts, registration or frozen worker command. The separately registered driver must perform those checks, bind actual operating limits and stage identities, anchor output paths, and preserve source-specific capability boundaries. Its receipt records operations and closed failure causes, not physical validity or probability premises. A measured-RSS operational clarification remains necessary before any production execution under stronger peak-cap wording in a scientific protocol.
