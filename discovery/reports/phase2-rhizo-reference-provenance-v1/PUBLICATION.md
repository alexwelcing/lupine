# Public documentary provenance checkpoint

Start with the [corrected decision and source assessment](README.md),
[43-location roster](reference-provenance.csv), [bibliography](bibliography.json)
and [actual exposure ledger](exposure-ledger.json). The
[original 36-ID, 172-edge graph](original-reference-frame.json) and
[completed public projection receipt](projection-public.json) are exact copies.
No raw reference object, private selected projection, full source notes or
property/performance values are included.

The [scope correction](scope-clarification.json) separates a descriptive census
of fixed stored labels from historical binder reconstruction and source-value
agreement. Exact paired stored labels, completeness and a stated estimand remain
unestablished; neither a census nor G21 admission is claimed. The
[additive independent review](independent-review/scope-correction-review.json)
binds this final wording. The [earlier review](independent-review/final-review.json)
is preserved as a historical review of the previous draft, including its missed
overrestriction; its old binding is not a final-source approval.

[Source bindings](source-bindings.json) retain original cache identities.
The [original-byte map](original-byte-map.json) resolves every published original
to either an exact copy or a reversible base64 wrapper. Historical README files
are wrapped to preserve their exact bytes without creating misleading relative
navigation. Original hashes apply to their decoded payloads. No source content
was silently normalized. Preparation/checklist waiting states and earlier drafts
remain dated history, not the current operational status.

The [initial promotion inventory](publication-history/initial-inventory.json)
is retained as history. Its paths and hashes describe the locally preserved first
promotion tree, before the root scope correction. It is not this final tree's
inventory. The later publication inventory is supplied separately to the root
publisher. The two navigation wrappers and this entrypoint change presentation
only; no private source was reread for the correction or packaging.

All selected notes were exposed to both documentary readers. A second reviewer
is not a second scientific sample. No links were followed, no numerical outcome
comparison or fit ran, and no follow-on stored-label operation is authorized by
this report. Root owns any separately scoped next operation and publication.
