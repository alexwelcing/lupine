The fixed question is recovery of recorded tensile yield strength at exactly
25°C, subject to recorded total elongation at least a development-only lower
median. The metadata scope is 314 original rows and 75 exact source-token groups.
It is a source-token holdout of archived database tuples; it does not establish
novel chemistry, independent studies, same-specimen pairing, measurement
uncertainty or plant suitability. Only archived experimental records are used;
no new DFT is proposed.

The exact source-balanced five-neighbor ranker, processing and objective-only
controls, twenty hash controls, fixed budgets and all gate thresholds are
specified before performance access. Thirty-five development groups are selected
by a fixed rule; the remaining forty receive one recorded random permutation
into twenty calibration and twenty evaluation panels. No real allocation has
been computed. Failed or interrupted allocation cannot be silently replaced.

## Custody sequence and remaining implementation

After final registration/CI: allocation and feature linkage precede development
targets; development fit and every full ranking freeze before any reserved
numeric decoder; role-blind completeness of all forty reserved panels precedes
calibration; all future pools and schedules seal before policy reveals; full truth
audit follows all 960 completed policy runs. An undefined endpoint in any reserved
primary panel forces unsupported primary calibration. A separately declared
complete-record secondary population retains every group, including empty panels.

Stopped and full primary orders are identical through the stopping cost, so they
cannot establish different discovery value at equal spend. Ranking value and
stopping/pool usefulness have separate, jointly required gates. All original
denominators, incomplete truth, vacuous retention and negative results remain.
