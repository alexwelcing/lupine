## Behavior and scientific scope

The [workbench contract](../docs/decision-pool-workbench-contract.md) defines
complete calibration panels, one future ranked panel without future labels,
explicit protocol premises, exact risk arithmetic and all four versioned receipts.
Every calibration panel remains in the denominator. Missing labels, unsupported
premises and insufficient rank resolution preserve distinct whole-universe
fallbacks. Selection is sealed before separately bound future outcomes are read.
Replay audits the actual selected prefix, every feasible tied minimum when truth
is complete, and unresolved truth otherwise. A zero-size statistical pool does
not establish infeasibility.

Three added examples preserve a tied-optimum loss, incomplete-calibration
abstention and a finite empty pool that loses a feasible optimum. These are
constructed software controls, not empirical coverage estimates. The general
claim remains marginal across calibration and one future panel under a frozen
ranking and the required panel-score law. It does not condition on feasibility,
the realized calibration set, an individual material, or repeated adaptive use.
There is no feasibility, incumbent, regret or hard-interval certificate.

The wrapper bounds lexical size, nesting, scalar bit lengths, each panel and
the total number of candidates before exact-number/model construction. Linux
HTTP uses the existing bounded subprocess, retaining its CPU, wall, memory,
output and concurrency limits. The parent checks closed receipt shapes and
original identity bindings without reconstructing scientific objects. These
are finite engineering guards, not a general isolation theorem. CLI's generic
outer JSON limit precedes the narrower mode-specific profile; see the contract.
