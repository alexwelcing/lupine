# BAM selected-results workbook: preparation history

This route treats the selected workbook as an exposed metrology reference. It is permanently excluded from new blind predictive evaluation. The pinned metadata identifies one 54,960-byte workbook under record DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17846617, version 1.0, with record license CC BY 4.0. No acquisition had occurred when the following repairs and synthetic checks were made.

The first author reader invocation passed 11 synthetic tests. Independent source review then identified that an interruption before the acquisition receipt was written could leave no durable record of the reserved request. The original scope, runner, reader, tests and first author receipt are retained under `before-request-slot-repair/`. The repaired runner writes an exclusive request reservation and syncs the file and directory before requesting the fixed URL. A reservation is not evidence that a request completed or even began. The next author invocation passed 13 tests, including injected interruption and sync failure; its source differs from the original invocation.

The first independent pure-reader invocation ran 34 tests and had four failures: three demonstrated source defects (UTF-16LE without a BOM could bypass the intended UTF-8/declaration guard, with and without a DOCTYPE; a neutral-named OLE part escaped the active-content type check). The fourth was a reviewer fixture expectation of eight parsed parts where nine were correct. The reviewer retains that exact invocation and distinguishes its fixture correction from source repairs. Focused source review also found duplicate direct text components and inline-string content on non-inline cells could be silently combined or ignored.

The original pre-reader-repair scope and source remain under `before-reader-review-repair/`. The repair rejects decoded NUL before the XML parser; rejects OLE/embedded-package content types; and rejects duplicate/nested admitted text, unexpected inline-string components and nested scalar content. This is a bounded projection, not a full OOXML schema validator. The final-source author invocation passed 16 methods in 0.057 seconds of unittest time (0.378219 seconds process elapsed), with all bound files unchanged. Every author invocation used synthetic bytes and stubbed requests only; no actual source or network was accessed.

Acquisition remains conditional on an independent review of the final held scope, reader and runner. If the eventual request or parser fails, that first failure is retained; no retry, alternate source, codec fallback or reader widening is automatic. Measurements, strings, formulas and stored formula caches may be exposed to the machine/researcher under the separate documentary scope, while no formulas, macros, applications or external links execute. Structural counts and passing synthetic tests are not independent specimen counts, uncertainty validation or scientific evidence of predictive superiority.
