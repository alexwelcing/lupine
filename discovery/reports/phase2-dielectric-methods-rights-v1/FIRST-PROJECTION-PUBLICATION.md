# Exact first projection, with its exposure limitation

Root requested that the first qualitative-exposure projection be preserved in the public evidence where licensing permits. The [lossless UTF-8 wrapper](public-evidence/first-projection.utf8.json) contains the exact original generated projection, not the article XML. Decode its `text` field as UTF-8 to recover those original bytes and verify the declared size and SHA256.

The embedded prose is a **modified excerpt** of Petousis et al., *High-throughput screening of inorganic compounds for the discovery of novel dielectric and optical materials*, Scientific Data 4, 160134 (2017), DOI [10.1038/sdata.2016.134](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.134), available as [PMC5315501](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5315501/). Crossref's version-of-record metadata and the primary article's front-matter link identify [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) for the article. This attribution and article license do not establish a license for any separately hosted dataset.

Modifications made by the original projector: it selected the Methods and Data Records descendant prose, omitted table/figure/formula and reference-marker subtrees, normalized whitespace, and replaced digit runs with `[NUMBER]`. Formulas can therefore be incomplete and the projection is not an authoritative standalone rendering of the paper. No article figures or table cells are embedded in this wrapper. Generated source locators, metadata and exposure fields are also included exactly.

The Data Records selection included nested qualitative results and screening discussion. The original boolean denying full result display is too broad when interpreted as denying all qualitative results exposure; the [exposure ledger](exposure-ledger.json) explicitly corrects that interpretation. Publication preserves the first projection rather than repairing or hiding it, and does not undo the researcher's exposure. No label values were recovered or model choices made from it.

The prospective acquisition scope originally excluded broad prose excerpts from publication. This later, explicit publication decision changes only whether the already generated modified excerpt is copied as evidence; it authorizes no new source request or data inspection. Raw XML and all unselected machine-read response bodies remain excluded. No Dryad, DataCite, Figshare or observation-file request ran.
