## Exact accepted advisory

The alternative is sound as a **prospective disclosure-policy amendment**, with these limits.

Exact `allowed_texts` is a consistency check on previously known text. It is not required by the mathematics or inert parsing, and it cannot discover unknown dictionary definitions. The paper supports the two documentary role names; neither those names nor the published notebook inventory proves their contents are outcome-free.

Keep the existing **two runs × three phases**:

1. **A — layout:** unchanged custody, replay, package and complete two-role structural checks.
2. **B — documentary inventory:** enumerate both exact roles completely, exposing bounded plain inline/shared-string text with coordinates and storage types. Retain nontext cells structurally without their values. Preserve existing caps—including 1,024 projected fields, 2,048 characters each—and existing parser/storage/merge rules. Formula, unsupported text, dependency, cap or cleanup failures block the entire projection. No truncation, numerical filtering, record-sheet preview or automatic continuation.
3. **C — frame:** only after a newly committed typed binding is justified from the inventory and published evidence. No separate actual strict-documentary-B operation is needed.

For **C’s dependency validation**, require:

- Authenticate the exact A/B receipts and their original producers; bind their canonical hashes and the same immutable source.
- Recompute A and the complete inventory on C’s verified source buffer; compare their complete payloads exactly. Historical exposure flags remain historical.
- Validate every strict documentary binding against the recomputed inventory’s exact role, coordinate, storage and literal. Meaning/unit fields remain reviewed assertions.
- Reconstruct the strict documentary projection **inside C** before opening record sheets, preserving its original checks. Do not forge a historical strict-B receipt or relabel an inventory receipt as one.
- Then apply the frozen complete frame rules. Unsupported joins, unknown required meanings or unbound row classifications remain NO_GO; no freehand classification or observed-value selection.

B’s success must mean only **documentary text inventory complete**. Unknown text can include empirical examples or results. Record whole worksheet decoding, any whole shared-string decoding, and researcher-visible text separately. Record worksheets remain unopened during B, but their strings may share the decoded table. Therefore `fresh_status=UNESTABLISHED`; no outcome-free, scientific-admission or independence claim follows.

Preserve any incidental text exposure and all failures. No retries with broader bounds, changed roles or narrower post-observation selections. The unchanged `5fd1…` pure APIs remain historical; the amended profile needs its own explicit contract and tests before actual use.

## Why structural identity cannot establish semantic safety

Consider two hypothetical worksheets with the same exact documentary-role name,
relationships, cell coordinates, storage types, formula absence and merge layout.
Their one plain text cell is respectively `Yield strength is reported in MPa`
and `Observed yield strength for specimen Q was 900 MPa`. Both fit the same
bounded plain-string grammar. The second discloses a hypothetical observation;
the first supplies a definition. These are generated examples, not Li values.
Structural checks therefore cannot guarantee that newly disclosed text is free
of outcomes. Digit stripping is not a solution: `0.2% offset yield definition`
can be documentary, while an outcome can be written in words. The accepted
policy allows bounded role-scoped text discovery with explicit exposure; it
makes no automatic semantic classification or outcome-absence claim.

An exact-literal allowlist can reject the second string when only the first is
prebound, but that is a prior-content premise. It remains useful for C's
same-source strict reconstruction after B; it is not a general schema-discovery
mechanism. No mathematical coverage claim is obtained merely by replacing it.

## Evidence scope and implementation consequences

The published paper-methods report cites Data Records, Sec11, for separate
`References` and `Column Descriptions` worksheets. It also preserves the paper
1050/55 versus archive1070/54 mismatch and unknown actual joins. This is a
published documentary lead, not a finding from the actual workbook. The
published notebook verification describes a closed static API inventory whose
literal values remain withheld; it does not independently bind those role names
or prove execution/worksheet equivalence. The published schema-gate and layout
notes already distinguish machine decoding from researcher disclosure and warn
that named documentary roles may contain numerical examples.

All prior ZIP/OPC/workbook, custody, replay, reservation, cleanup and resource
limits remain required. B projects every admitted textual cell in the two fixed
roles, or blocks completely; it never selects the first 1024 cells. Nontext
values stay withheld and their structural positions remain accounted for.
Unsupported textual storage, formulas, required merged-text interpretation,
unknown dependencies and cap violations are not silently omitted. Whole shared
strings may be processed privately only within the existing decoder and bounds;
only text selected by the fixed role policy is disclosed. No source substitution,
formula execution, unit conversion or worksheet/style/comment/opaque-payload
fallback is introduced.

C should use an explicit `prior_inventory_sha256` dependency rather than the
legacy strict-documentary prior field. Its newly committed binding must be
validated before record access. Same-buffer recomputation and strict
reconstruction are current in-memory work, not invented historical operations.
A complete inventory still cannot justify record identities/classes that the
source evidence does not support. Such gaps stop C; this amendment supplies no
freehand row labels or automatic frame/admission.

For generated contract tests, challenge: same structure with definition versus
outcome-like text; a required field absent from inventory; wrong coordinate,
storage or literal; source/producer/prior hash drift; forged strict-B prior;
incomplete/truncated inventory; formulas or unsupported text/merge dependencies;
1024/1025 fields and 2048/2049 characters; unrelated shared-string canaries;
partial decode/cleanup failure; and any attempted record read before dependency
validation. These are proposed challenges, not tests executed by this advisory.

Root's chosen operation budget remains at most two fixed invocations each of
A, B-inventory and C. Strict known-literal B stays available as an unchanged pure
legacy API; it is not an extra actual phase. Reproduction is computational
replay, not an independent scientific sample. No automatic dispatch or broader
retry is implied.
